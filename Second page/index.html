Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of certifications or credentials will I receive upon completion?
Upon fulfilling all course requirements (attendance, exams, projects and more), you'll receive a certificate validating your skills. We are accredited by ACTD and are an approved trusted partner of Figma — our certificates are recognized globally.
What kind of courses does AppClick ICT Academy offer?
A wide range — Python, Java, JavaScript, PHP, web design, mobile app engineering, data science, data analytics, digital marketing, graphic design, UI/UX design and more.
Who can enroll in AppClick ICT Academy?
Regardless of background or prior experience, everyone interested in learning coding and digital skills is welcome — from complete beginners to experts sharpening their skill set.
What teaching methods does AppClick ICT Academy use?
A practical, hands-on approach: lectures, interactive coding exercises, projects, and real-world case studies in a safe, cooperative learning environment.
Are the instructors experienced in the industry?
Yes — our instructors are professionals with years of industry experience who bring real-world ideas and examples into the classroom.
Is there any flexibility in the course schedules?
Yes, we offer full-time and part-time options including evening and weekend classes to fit around work and other commitments.
How can I get started with AppClick ICT Academy?
